About the Evening

Songs that find the universal in the personal — and a room that feels like it was made for them.

Peter Kleinhans writes folk-pop with the warmth of Harry Chapin and the restless honesty of Neil Young. His path to the stage wound through racetracks, courtrooms, and the heartland of America — and every mile of it finds its way into the songs. This is an evening of music, stories, and the kind of connection that only happens live.

"Kleinhans stands at the center of an unusually wide-ranging Venn diagram — a deeply attentive observer."
